    How it works
    Vt = OCV(z) − R0I − V1 − V2,  I includes the short current Vt/Rsc
    dVk/dt = I/Ck − Vk/(RkCk),  m cp dT/dt = Joule heat − hA(T − Tamb)

    Resistances follow Arrhenius temperature terms and grow with ageing. The alarm fires when the 30 s average residual stays below the threshold for 10 s; the residual's slope at rest then gives the short's current and resistance (the abnormal-charge-depletion idea, e.g. Hu, Wei and He, 2021). The safety score multiplies temperature, voltage and short sub-scores, after Cabrera-Castillo et al. (2016). Parameters are illustrative.

    The one electrical signal

    Live-cell voltage in the first 1 s sample after the nail. At 1 Hz the collapse itself is invisible: the gap I want to work on.

    Rig design decisions

    Two-part holderOne geometry for three setups, fixed 7 mm gap.
    Clearance-fit boresThermocouples reach mid-cell without crushing.
    Zero-gap clampA repeatable worst case, with a voltage-lead port.
    Instrumented dummiesEpoxy-aluminium fill, sensor embedded in the core.
    PLA on purposeFast, cheap iteration; fixture damage accepted.

    Honest limits

    • Dummy neighbours were uncharged: heat delivered, not cascade.
    • No sensor on the live cell; 3 trials per setup.
    • Open lab, 4–17 °C starts; nail penetration only.

    What carries over

    • Safe, hands-on destructive cell testing.
    • Keeping messy trials and stating the limitation.
    • Reporting against the right baseline, with spread.
